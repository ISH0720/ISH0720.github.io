How did bubble tea develop?

Combining tea with dairy products has a long history in many regions. Different cultures have created their own unique styles of milk tea based on local tea leaves, dairy ingredients and eating habits. The modern bubble tea we know today is the result of continuous innovation built upon traditional tea drinks.

Timeline of Development

Traditional Period: The Combination of Tea and Milk

In some regions, people mixed tea with milk, cream or other dairy products to drink, forming diverse local food traditions.

20th Century: Modern Hong Kong-style Milk Tea

Hong Kong-style milk tea gradually developed its distinct features. It usually uses strong black tea with evaporated milk, delivering a smooth texture and a prominent tea flavour.

1980s: The Rise of Bubble Tea

Bubble tea with tapioca pearls was developed in Taiwan. It later spread around the world and became one of the most iconic tea-based drinks.

21st Century to Present: Era of Creative Tea Drinks

Bubble tea shops keep launching fruit teas, cheese foam toppings, fresh milk teas and various add-ins. Ordering methods and flavour choices have also become much more diverse.

Why is bubble tea becoming more and more popular?

Modern bubble tea lets customers customize sweetness, ice level, tea base and toppings to their taste. It is easy to buy and perfect for hanging out and chatting with friends, so it has gradually become a familiar daily drink for young people in many cities.
