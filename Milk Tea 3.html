China's Tea Drink Market

In recent years, tea drink brands in China have developed rapidly. Different brands attract consumers through product innovation, store design, price positioning and brand image. Below are some well-known examples.

HEYTEA

Known for cheese tea and creative drinks. It focuses on product research and youthful branding.

Nayuki

Serves tea and baked goods, including fresh fruit tea and fresh milk tea.

Mixue

Famous for affordable prices and wide store coverage. It sells ice cream, fruit tea and milk tea.

Chabaidao

Wide selection including milk tea, fruit tea and seasonal limited drinks.

Goodme

Focuses on freshly made tea, covering milk tea, fruit tea and more categories.

Yidiandian

It offers classic milk tea with customizable sweetness and ice level options.
