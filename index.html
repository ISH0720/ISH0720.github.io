What is bubble tea?

Bubble tea is usually made by mixing tea with milk, creamer or other dairy products. Depending on the recipe, it can have a strong milk flavour and fresh tea aroma. Toppings such as tapioca pearls, coconut jelly and pudding can also be added for different textures.

There are many types of bubble tea today: classic pearl milk tea, fruit tea, cheese foam tea, and drinks made with fresh milk and freshly brewed tea.

What does a bubble tea usually contain?

Tea base: black tea, green tea, oolong tea, etc., which creates the main tea flavour.

Milk & sweetness: fresh milk, creamer or plant‑based milk, plus syrup or other sweeteners.

Toppings: tapioca pearls, coconut jelly, grass jelly, pudding, etc., to enrich the texture.

Common Types of Bubble Tea

Classic Bubble Tea

Made mainly with tea and milk. It has a rich flavour and is where many people first get to know bubble tea.

Tapioca Bubble Tea

Added chewy tapioca pearls. It combines tea aroma, milk flavour and a fun texture.

Fruit Tea

Based on tea with fresh fruit or fruit juice. It tastes light and refreshing with many choices.
